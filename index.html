About Rutherford

An old university
still becoming new.

Rutherford is home to students from every kind of background— legacy families, scholarship students, athletes, artists, researchers, and people who have not yet decided who they want to become.

Tradition matters here. So does everything that comes after it.

Rutherford University is one of the country's oldest and most respected private universities. Its historic campus, academic traditions, and student life have grown together for generations.

The university is particularly known for its programs in political science, international relations, law, economics, business, and the humanities.

“Tradition, Inquiry, Influence.” Tradition gives us a foundation. Inquiry asks us to question it. Influence asks what we do next.

Rutherford Day

The university's own anniversary. There are no classes. Instead, the campus fills with student council games, faculty-versus-student matches, club booths, picnics, food trucks, vintage markets, exhibitions, outdoor films, and music.

More than anything, it is known as the day students spread blankets across Central Lawn and spend the entire day with the people around them.

Rutherford Raiders

Lacrosse
is tradition.

Lacrosse is Rutherford's signature sport. Home games transform the atmosphere of the entire campus, bringing students, alumni, and generations of supporters into the stadium.

The sport is particularly associated with Rutherford's long-established families and athletic traditions, but game days belong to everyone.
